Philippine Science Centrum

📍 Marikina City, Philippines

The Philippine Science Centrum makes learning science an active experience. Its hands-on exhibits allow visitors to test ideas, observe scientific principles, and understand how science connects with everyday life.

What Can You Find Inside?

  • Hands-on science exhibits
  • Interactive demonstrations of scientific principles
  • Learning activities about everyday science

Visitor reminder: Exhibits, opening hours, ticket prices, and available activities may change. Check the official website before your visit.
