Omniverse Museum

📍 Makati City, Philippines

Omniverse Museum brings science fiction and pop culture into one exciting space. Visitors can discover movie and comic-book memorabilia, costumes, props, and life-sized displays inspired by popular fictional worlds.

What Can You Find Inside?

  • Movie and pop-culture displays
  • Costumes, props, and collectibles
  • Life-sized character and themed installations

Visitor reminder: Exhibits, opening hours, ticket prices, and available activities may change. Check the official website before your visit.
