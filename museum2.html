Art in Paradise

📍 Pattaya, Thailand

Art in Paradise is a 3D art gallery filled with optical illusions and large realistic paintings. Visitors can step into the scenes, interact with the artwork, and become part of creative photo experiences.

What Can You Find Inside?

  • 3D paintings and perspective illusions
  • Themed art rooms and creative photo scenes
  • Interactive artworks that make visitors part of the picture

Visitor reminder: Exhibits, opening hours, ticket prices, and available activities may change. Check the official website before your visit.
