teamLab Planets TOKYO

📍 Tokyo, Japan

teamLab Planets is a multisensory art experience where visitors enter large-scale digital installations. Its themed spaces combine light, sound, water, and movement to create an immersive journey through art and nature.

What Can You Find Inside?

  • Immersive digital art spaces
  • Water and light-based installations
  • Large-scale sensory environments

Visitor reminder: Exhibits, opening hours, ticket prices, and available activities may change. Check the official website before your visit.
